Shijie Zhou

Fiery red, forever fearless — Sunset, Caroline Polachek

I am Shijie Zhou, a Master’s student in Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence at the Technical University of Munich. I work on robot learning, embodied intelligence and learning-based control, towards general-purpose robots that act reliably in the real world.

The question that drives me: how can we build a scalable robot-learning flywheel that unifies perception, whole-body control and dexterous manipulation? Answering it takes the whole stack, so I am becoming a full-stack roboticist, from hardware and control to high-level models.

Away from the lab, I enjoy music and exploring the world. Check out my photography gallery→
